# Moving, Duplicating and Selecting

How to move, duplicate and multi-select clips in the grid, and how to name clips.

## Moving

- Drag a clip's name strip (below the thumbnail) to another slot to move it. It can go to another layer.
- You can also `⌘`-drag a slot to move it (from anywhere on the thumbnail).
  - If the destination has a clip, the two are swapped.
  - Playing clips don't stop.

## Duplicating

`⌥`-drag a name strip or slot to duplicate it.

- It becomes a separate clip with the same settings.
- It can go to another layer.
- If the destination has a clip, it is replaced.
- It can't be dropped onto a playing clip.

## Adding to cues

Drop a name strip onto the list in the “Cues” panel to add it as a cue. → [Cues](cues.md)

## Multi-selecting

`⇧`-click slots to select several clips (click again to deselect; this doesn't play them).

- `⌘`-drag one of the selected clips to move them all together, keeping their arrangement. If another clip is in the way, nothing moves.
- Press Delete, or right-click, to delete them all.
- Right-click to move them to the same position in another deck.
- A normal click or switching decks clears the selection.

## Renaming

Rename a clip with “Rename…” in its right-click menu, or with “Name” at the very top of the clip tab in the parameter panel.

- Videos, still images and generators can all be named (the file name or generator type is unchanged).
- The name appears on the slot's name strip, in the clip tab, in the playing display and in cues.
- Clearing it (× button) restores the original name.
- It is kept when duplicating, and saved with the project.
