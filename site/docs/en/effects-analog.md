# Analog Gear Effects

Values of the effects in the "Analog Gear" category, which imitate old analog video equipment.

## Glitch Box

Disrupts the image like circuit-bent gear.

- Sync loss: each line shifts sideways, and bands sometimes tear.
- Vertical roll.
- Sudden jumps: bands jump, and the whole image jumps vertically.
- Color bleed and color shift: only the color lags, flowing from the left.
- Gain: too much saturates the image.
- Foldover: bright areas invert into stripes.
- Enhancer: edges glow double.
- Color oscillation: colors oscillate into rainbows.
- Noise: static and white lines.
- Scanlines.
- Glitch speed.

With the knobs at 0 and Gain at 100%, the image is unchanged. It also works well on an FX pad, disrupting only while held.

## VHS Tape

- Tracking error (noise bands roll by)
- Bottom noise (head switching)
- Wobble
- Color bleed
- Fading
- Dropouts (white lines)
- Noise
- Quality (horizontal resolution)

## Video Feedback

Keeps layering the previous frame, as if filming a monitor that shows its own camera.

- Applied to the previous frame: zoom, rotation, offset, hue shift. All are per second (same speed at any frame rate).
- Persistence.
- Key brightness: dark areas become see-through, showing the previous image.
- Blend: Overlay on top, Add, Brighter one.
- Contrast, blur.

Works especially well with images that have transparency, such as text or shapes.

## Raster Scan

Rutt/Etra style. Splits the image into horizontal scanlines and lifts the lines where it is brighter. The space between the lines is transparent.

Values: Lines, Lift height, Line width, Tilt back, Color (Original color, 1 color, Monochrome), brightness, Wave, Original image below, Hide lines behind.

## CRT Monitor

- Scanlines
- Phosphor grain (RGB stripes) and its size
- Screen curvature (corners are rounded and black)
- Glow
- Afterglow (bright things leave trails)
- Vignette
- Brightness
- Flicker

## Film

Adds the look of old film (in the "Analog Gear" category). Grain, scratches and flicker change at the frame rate.

Values: Grain (noise on brightness), Grain size (px at 1080p), Vignette, Vignette size, Scratches (thin vertical lines now and then), Dust (black specks now and then), Flicker (overall brightness changes a little each frame), Shake (the frame jumps up and down a little), Fading (lower saturation, toward sepia), Frame rate (6 to 60 fps).

With everything except Frame rate at 0, the image is unchanged.
