# Camera

How to place video from a camera or capture device as a clip, using the generator “From Camera”.

## Supported devices

- Webcam
- HDMI capture
- iPhone (Continuity Camera)

## Placing

Place the generator “From Camera” (category “From Other Apps / Cameras”) in a slot, then choose the device under “Camera” in the clip tab.

- The list updates when devices are connected or disconnected.
- The first time, permission to use the camera is requested.

## Values

- Scaling: Fit, Fill, Stretch.
- Flip horizontally (mirror).
- Density.

## How cameras are opened

However many slots the same camera is placed in, it is opened only once, and it is closed after 10 seconds of not being used.
